# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to participate in our community without harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project defines a code of conduct. It applies in the project and in public spaces when people represent the project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment:

- Respectful language
- Acceptance of different points of view
- Constructive criticism
- Focus on the community
- Empathy toward other members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insults, and personal attacks
- Harassment in public or in private
- Publishing private information without permission
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would call unsuitable in a professional environment

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for the standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers by opening an issue or a pull request. All reports are reviewed. The identity of the reporter is kept confidential.

This Code of Conduct applies in the project and in community spaces. It applies when a person is part of the project community.
